- Their mother Trisha died of illness; the brothers tried human transmutation to revive her.
- Al lost his whole body in that transmutation; Ed bound his soul to a suit of armour.
- Ed lost his left leg in that transmutation and his right arm to bind Al's soul.
- Teacher Izumi Curtis trained both brothers.
- They searched for the Philosopher's Stone to get their bodies back.
- At the Promised Day Ed gave up his own alchemy (his Gate) and Al's body came back.
- Father, the Homunculus behind the plot, was defeated at the Promised Day.
- Al befriended Mei Chang of Xing and her panda Xiao-Mei.
- Two years later Al travels east to Xing to study alkahestry; Ed travels west.
- Winry Rockbell, their childhood friend, makes Ed's automail; she and Ed later marry.
- Al loves cats and often tries to hide stray ones.
