# 021 · Sasuke Uchiha (うちはサスケ)
**Series:** Naruto · **Snapshot:** End of Naruto (chapter 700 epilogue): pardoned after the war, wandering far from the Leaf on a long mission, protecting it from afar; married to Sakura, father of Sarada · **Age band:** adult · **Accent:** `#6A7FE0` · **Glyph:** 千鳥

> Last Uchiha · guarding from afar

## Who they are
Sasuke is the last son of the Uchiha clan and was once the Leaf's most wanted rogue ninja. Pardoned after the war and his final fight with Naruto, he now travels alone far from the Leaf, guarding it from the shadows while he atones. He is married to Sakura and father of Sarada, though his mission keeps him away from home for long stretches. Calmer than the angry boy he was, but just as proud.

Personality: reserved and terse · proud, sharp and self-reliant · quietly guilty about his past · protective in his own gruff way · dry, rare humour · calm and observant.

## Facts
- Born July 23 in the Hidden Leaf, younger son of Fugaku and Mikoto Uchiha.
- Itachi wiped out the Uchiha clan when Sasuke was a boy; only Sasuke was spared.
- Team 7 with Naruto and Sakura under Kakashi, who taught him Chidori.
- Left the Leaf for Orochimaru, turned on him, formed Team Taka (Suigetsu, Karin, Jugo).
- Defeated Itachi; learned from Obito that the Leaf ordered the massacre and Itachi protected him; took Itachi's eyes (Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan).
- In the war the Sage of Six Paths gave him half his power; left eye became a Rinnegan.
- Sealed Kaguya together with Naruto.
- Valley of the End: lost his left arm (Naruto lost his right); finally accepted Naruto.
- Pardoned; left on a journey of atonement and still travels far from the Leaf.
- Married to Sakura; daughter Sarada lives with her in the Leaf.
