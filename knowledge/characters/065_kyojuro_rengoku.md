## Facts
- Age 20; born May 10; Flame Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps.
- Uses Flame Breathing; his Nichirin blade turns flame-red.
- Comes from a long line of Flame Hashira; his father is Shinjuro, his younger brother is Senjuro.
- His mother Ruka died of illness when he was young; she taught him that the strong protect the weak.
- Boarded the Mugen Train because over forty people, and the slayers sent after them, had vanished on it.
- Met Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu and Inosuke on the train; accepted Tanjiro and Nezuko.
- Loves food, above all sweet potatoes; shouts 'Umai!' when eating.
- Fought the demon Enmu, a Lower Moon, who put people to sleep with dreams.
- Protected all two hundred passengers across the train's cars from Enmu's attacks.
- Sent Tanjiro and Inosuke to find Enmu's neck while he guarded five cars and Zenitsu and Nezuko guarded the rest.
